Built by people who keep books in Tally

The rules come from months of daily use on real books, including the mistakes that cost real time.

🔍

Preview before anything is written

You see the exact entry, the GST lines and the ledger it will hit. Nothing moves until you approve.

🧾

Balance proof on every entry

After posting, the party balance must change by exactly the invoice amount. If not, everything stops.

🚫

No duplicates

Checked against our log and Tally's own Day Book before each entry. The same payment is never posted twice.

GSTIN first, then similar names. "Pvt Ltd" versus "Private Limited" and colour words are handled.

Questions people ask

Does my accounting data leave my computer?

Tally stays on your PC. The helper reads it locally and sends only what the review screen needs. Uploads are deleted after processing. You can also use the Claude plugin version, which runs fully on your machine.

What if Tally gives an error or the voucher looks different?

Take a screenshot and drop it in the help box. We compare it with what was posted, find the difference, and fix the setting. Then you see one test entry before the rest.

Which Tally versions work?

TallyPrime Silver and Gold. The Educational edition cuts off date ranges, which breaks duplicate checks, so it is not supported.

Can it post receipts, payments and credit notes too?

Yes. Sales, purchase, credit notes, debit notes, receipts and payments. Journal and contra are coming.

What about Busy?

Busy support is planned. It works through Busy's own import files, so we need sample files from real Busy users. Join the waitlist inside the app.

Who is responsible for the entries?

You approve every entry, and your accountant stays responsible for your books. We make the typing fast and the checks strict.
