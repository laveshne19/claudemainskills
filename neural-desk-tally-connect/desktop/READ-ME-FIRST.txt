TALLY CONNECT, DESKTOP TEST BUILD  (version 0.2.0)
By Neural Desk

What this is
  A small program that runs on the Windows PC that has TallyPrime. You open it in your browser, upload an Excel or CSV of
  invoices, bills, credit notes, debit notes, receipts or payments, review what it found, and post. Every entry is checked against
  the party balance in Tally afterwards.

Try it first, with no Tally at all
  1. Double click  INSTALL.bat  (once).
  2. Double click  Start Demo (no Tally needed).bat
  3. In the browser page that opens, press "Use the demo sample" and walk through it. A simulated Tally is used.

Then test with your real Tally
  1. Open TallyPrime and your company. F1 (Help), Settings, Connectivity, Client/Server: set "TallyPrime acts as" to Server or Both, port 9000.
  2. Double click  Start Tally Connect.bat
  3. On the Setup tab: Test connection, fill in the company GSTIN and state, press "Scan my Tally", check the ledger names, Save.
  4. Switch ON "Read only mode" for the first run. Read a real file on the Import tab and check the review table against what you know.
  5. Switch Read only OFF. Post ONE invoice. Open it in Tally and look at it carefully.
  6. Follow LIVE-TEST-CHECKLIST.md in the docs folder.

If anything looks different from your own entries
  Take a screenshot (Windows key + Shift + S). On the Help tab press "Copy diagnostics". Send both to Claude with a line on what you expected.

Important
  This build was tested against a simulated Tally, not yet against your real one. Test on a COPY of your company first if you can.
  PDFs and photos are read by Claude with the Tally Connect plugin (folder: plugin). This screen handles Excel and CSV.
